---
name: lab-discuss
description: Discuss a teaching need, investigate existing behavior and help the learner make explicit design decisions before implementation.
---

Use this when the learner has an idea with unresolved behavior or scope.

1. Read the request and docs/learning-rules.txt. Investigate relevant project
   facts yourself. Explain only findings that affect a design choice, in plain
   language; distinguish observations from assumptions.
2. Interview the learner in short rounds of at most three independent questions.
   For each real choice, recommend an option and explain its consequence. Ask
   later-dependent questions after their prerequisites are decided.
3. Use concrete given/action/result examples to test the shared understanding.
   Identify preserved behavior, a boundary and an out-of-scope item. Existing
   facts belong to the agent; product choices belong to the learner.
4. Summarize the choices for confirmation. Save confirmed decisions and reasons
   to workshop/decisions.txt using templates/decisions.txt. Keep open items marked.

Complete when the learner has confirmed a record sufficient to plan the first
small change. This Skill ends with decisions; implementation belongs to a later
task. Instructor examples are available only when the learner requests them.
