# Learning Lab

A course space where learners read lessons and practise, and teachers want to
maintain the material used for learning.

## Language

**Course**: A named learning experience containing ordered chapters.
**Chapter**: An ordered group of lessons within one course.
**Lesson**: A unit of learning content within a chapter. It may include examples,
resources or knowledge checks; these are optional.
**Knowledge check**: A question attached to a lesson for a learner to try.
**Attempt**: One valid submitted answer to a knowledge check.
**First-attempt score**: The original result of the learner's first valid quiz
answer to each question; later practice does not replace that result.
**Reading completion**: A learner's mark that they finished a lesson; distinct
from a quiz mark.
**Content maintenance**: A teacher's work to keep teaching material usable and
current. Its editing, review and visibility rules are choices for the new feature.
