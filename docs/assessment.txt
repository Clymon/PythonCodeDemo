OBSERVABLE WORKSHOP OUTCOME

The participant can operate the course platform and state the teaching goal;
judge one Agent finding against evidence; choose a solution with a reason and
scope boundary; approve a complete usable slice; operate its delivered result;
and explain what the checks establish and what remains unverified.

A meaningful result is one verified teaching-to-learner path, not a complete LMS
content-management system. Human decisions and evidence matter more than a fixed
feature count, handwritten code, source-file recall or a coverage percentage.
