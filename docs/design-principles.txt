PRINCIPLES BEFORE METHODS

A tracer bullet delivers one small, observable outcome through the layers it
needs. For a learner-selected review list, mark a lesson, leave it, and find it
again. Choose the stopping point and its check before building the next step.

A deep module offers a small interface to useful behavior. In Learning Lab,
retrieving a course hides traversal of chapters, safe projection of practice
questions and progress calculations. Discuss the behavior that callers need
and the checks that will still work if its implementation changes.
