LEARNING LAB — improve a familiar course platform with an AI Agent

Use student. The instructor demonstrates, then participants practise independently.
The prepared starting product is a working English LMS with courses, chapters,
lessons, optional examples/resources/checks and persistent course learning state.
Teachers cannot yet maintain content through the interface. That is the shared
business goal; the participant still chooses the solution and first usable slice.

1. OPERATE A COURSE
   Open My courses, enter a course, use the chapter directory and read a lesson.
   Try a check, mark a lesson complete and inspect My progress. Observe that
   ordinary reading can work without code or a quiz. Do not study the sample
   syllabus as the training's own course content.

2. EXPLORE FOR A TEACHING PURPOSE
   Use the situation in docs/agent-exploration.txt: a teacher has a lesson plan
   and two questions and wants students to learn with them in this platform.
   Ask the Agent what works already, what is missing and what first change helps.
   It investigates technical facts and explains their teaching consequences.
   Judge one supported claim and one recommendation, rather than reciting files.

3. DISCUSS REAL CHOICES — grill-me when useful
   Use docs/client-brief.txt. Discuss a small set of credible approaches.
   Examples: maintain an existing lesson or add new material; edit in the page
   or import; show saved material immediately or separate preparation/publication.
   Investigate facts rather than asking the human to know the code. The human
   chooses product behavior, scope, trade-offs and acceptance examples.
   Confirm shared understanding; do not silently adopt the instructor reference.

4. MAKE A READY PIECE OF WORK
   Use to-spec to synthesize the discussion and agree a useful test interface.
   Explain tracer bullets before to-tickets: one narrow COMPLETE usable behavior,
   through the layers it needs, with real blockers and a size a fresh Agent can handle.
   Use to-tickets to propose a breakdown; approve a first task that a teacher and
   learner can actually use. No fixed C01/C02 sequence is prescribed to participants.
   The configured local tracker stores spec/tickets under .scratch/<feature>/.

5. LET THE AGENT IMPLEMENT
   Use implement on an approved ready task. It uses tdd where possible at agreed
   interfaces, checks regularly, runs the final suite, calls code-review and commits.
   Deep modules: a clear operation hides useful behavior; tests exercise the agreed
   interface so internal changes can preserve them. Try the actual teaching-to-
   student workflow, read its evidence and accept or request a correction.

6. PRESERVE WHAT THE NEXT TASK NEEDS
   Use handoff when useful. A fresh recipient reconstructs the chosen scope,
   visibility rules, completed result, evidence and next action before editing.
   Keep durable decisions and references, rather than depending on chat memory.

7. CHOOSE A METHOD FOR A REAL NEXT QUESTION
   prototype: e.g. should saving expose a change to students or keep it as a draft?
   Make the design question clickable, record a verdict and preserve its experiment
   on a throwaway branch outside main. No production persistence or test suite in it.
   improve-codebase-architecture: investigate real friction and visual candidates;
   the human selects one or no change. A small app may need no refactor.
   retro: inspect the actual coding session and existing checks before proposing
   useful environment improvements. Practise one method meaningfully; demonstrate
   the other paths. These are choices, not a compulsory sequence of every Skill.

Show one design decision, a complete task, the actual usable result, evidence and
one limit. Source-file memorization, handwritten Python and a coverage threshold
are not completion criteria. Main's text editor is one prepared reference, not a
required student design and not a recorded WorkBuddy implementation.
