THREE-HOUR WORKSHOP — original Matt Skills, WorkBuddy and Python
14:30–17:30, individual practice after instructor demonstrations.

Learning outcome
Turn a teaching need into clear decisions and small, verifiable tasks; select an
original Skill to guide an agent; judge its result using observable evidence.

14:30–15:00  1. Environment and working product (30 min)
WorkBuddy, Git, student clone, prepared Python startup and browser tour.
Evidence: participant opens and operates the starting product.

15:00–15:15  2. Agent loop and first delegation (15 min)
Model, tools/harness, workspace, permissions, context and feedback through one
visible greeting change. Evidence: checked result and a local Git checkpoint.

15:15–15:35  3. Discuss a teaching need (20 min)
grill-me calls grilling. Agent investigates facts; human chooses rules and scope.
Evidence: confirmed choices and a concrete example in the discussion/optional notes.

15:35–15:55  4. Specification and complete tickets (20 min)
to-spec synthesizes the conversation; to-tickets proposes usable vertical slices.
Human judges acceptance, granularity and dependencies. Evidence: spec and ready ticket.

15:55–16:05  Break (10 min)

16:05–16:40  5. Implementation and feedback (35 min)
implement uses tdd and code-review; observe one meaningful red/green behavior,
checks and a commit. Evidence: running slice and human acceptance/revision decision.

16:40–16:55  6. Project rules and context (15 min)
AGENTS.md, original Skills, glossary, spec/tickets and continuity. Demonstrate
handoff for another teacher, with a recipient reconstruction check.

16:55–17:20  7. Review and acceptance (25 min)
Standards/Spec review, agreed examples, browser behavior, useful test gaps and
backend coverage limits. Evidence: a reasoned acceptance record.

17:20–17:30  Demonstration and transfer (10 min)
Show one decision, ticket and verified result. Propose a small tool improvement
in your own subject. No opening pretest or mandatory pairing.

Total: 180 minutes including the break. Prepare software, original dependencies
and the local issue-tracker configuration before class. Installation/setup slides
remain pre-class support. Do not require every participant to finish the entire
instructor feature; require a genuine verified slice and a clear next action.
The proposed timing is a teaching budget; validate pace through the rehearsal.
