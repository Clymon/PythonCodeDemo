LMS STARTING PRODUCT — navigate when investigation needs it

app.py starts one local Flask process. learning_lab/__init__.py loads course content
and initializes storage. catalog.py is the shared reading interface over the course,
chapter and lesson hierarchy. routes.py exposes course/navigation/learning behavior.
quiz.py holds existing scoring rules; db.py stores learner progress and attempts.
The English interface is templates/index.html and static/app.js/app.css. Local
resources need no CDN, build step or cloud account.

Trace a real teaching flow before proposing changes. The participant describes a
teaching purpose; the Agent investigates source facts and relevant checks, then
explains reusable capabilities, missing capabilities and meaningful consequences.

On student, content is read-only and loaded once at startup. A future maintenance
workflow needs a deliberate persistence/visibility decision. Do not mutate stable
IDs or quiz policy as a side effect of editing text. Main adds one prepared local
text-maintenance reference. See instructor/content-authoring/ only when a reference
is requested; it is not the learner's approved specification.
