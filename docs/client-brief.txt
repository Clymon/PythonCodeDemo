PLATFORM IMPROVEMENT — teachers maintain their own content

I have a lesson plan and two practice questions. I want my students to complete
that learning in this platform. Later I need to maintain my material myself,
without editing program files.

Help me understand what the platform can already support, what teachers and
students would do, and what is missing. Investigate facts yourself. Explain
options through their effect on teaching and learning. Recommend a useful first
step, ask me for decisions you cannot discover and wait for shared understanding.

The shared goal is not a prescribed feature list. An editor, import workflow,
new-lesson workflow and draft/publish behavior are possible choices, not already
approved requirements. Agree scope and evidence with the human before building.
