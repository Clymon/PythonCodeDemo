CHOOSE A TEACHING WISH

Instructor example
“I’d like to find the lessons I want to revisit.”

Other starting points
“I remember a keyword. Help me find the lesson that mentions it.”
“I’d like to see which questions I got right on a later try.”

You may write your own wish. Keep it to one sentence. Use /grill-me so the
Agent can check the existing app and ask you about meaningful choices.

For the instructor wish, what earns a place in a review list? A student might
choose a lesson, or a quiz result might suggest one. Can a completed lesson
still matter for review? Your decision determines what the app should do.
