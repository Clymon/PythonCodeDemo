EXPLORE THE PLATFORM FOR A TEACHING PURPOSE

First operate it: My courses -> Course -> Content -> Lesson. Open a reading-only
lesson and a lesson with questions. Try a question and mark a lesson complete.
Use My progress to see what the student can see afterwards. These sample lessons
are product data, not the syllabus that workshop participants must study.

Situation: I have a lesson plan and two practice questions. I want my students to
complete that learning in this platform and I want to maintain my material myself.

1. Get oriented with the Agent
   Help me understand this platform as a teacher. What can students do now, how
   is learning organized, and which parts of my teaching arrangement can it use?

2. Investigate the intended improvement in the same useful discussion
   Please check what is already possible, what teachers and students would do,
   what is missing, and two simple ways to move forward. Explain their effects
   on teaching work. Recommend a first step. Investigate before changing anything.

These are example situations, not scripts to memorize. Ask a follow-up that
matters to your intended lesson. The Agent chooses which files, tools, existing
checks or browser observations provide evidence. Technical discovery belongs to
the Agent; the participant does not need to ask where data loads or why a server
restarts. The Agent translates findings into capabilities, limits and consequences.

3. Make a supported decision
   Check one important claim against the actual page or evidence the Agent used.
   Explain why its recommended first step would help and what choice is still open.
   Keep that useful context for the requirements discussion; do not implement yet.

Observe real Thinking/file/tool activity as it happens. A tool label is not proof
of a test run. No compulsory sub-agent, greeting edit or staged AI failure.
The colored context illustration is conceptual, not an intercepted model request.
